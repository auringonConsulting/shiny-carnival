The work

What the work looks like

From my decade as the operator, in the seats this practice is built on.

A support queue that answers itself

In the product and ops seat at an early-stage platform, every user question landed on my team. I built automated support over the platform’s own data: routine answers instantly, judgment calls routed to a human. The team got their week back, and the queue stopped scaling with headcount.

Implementation, from a month to a week

At the same platform, enterprise customers signed, then waited. I mapped every step between signature and go-live, killed the ones that only existed by habit, and turned the rest into a process the team runs without me. Typical implementation went from more than a month to under a week without adding headcount.

A new service line on rails we already owned

As operating lead at a shared mobility startup, I inherited a matching platform built for one business: shared taxis at the region’s transit hubs. I found the second business in it, a van share on a corridor the transit map missed, and launched it with a national operator in weeks.
