For consultancies

I’m a management consultant with a product and operations background, specializing in transportation and regulatory programs. Firms bring me in for discovery, solutioning, project startup, and recovery, on short, defined engagements. I work under your firm’s name, and the client relationship stays yours.

Where it starts

What firms call about

A bid that needs domain depth. The RFP is in transportation, licensing, or regulation, and nobody on your team has run that kind of program from the agency side.

A discovery with no one to lead it. You won the discovery, and your bench is committed to delivery.

A new contract that needs a strong start. The award came through, and the first 90 days will set the tone for the whole project.

A project in trouble. The status report says green, and your delivery lead is worried.

Approach

How this works

I join as a subcontractor, under your firm’s name. Every engagement has a defined scope, specific deliverables, and a set end date. I work at the start of a project, around major launches, and when a project needs recovery. Your team runs the rest.

I’ve worked on every side of government programs: as an agency executive at NYC’s Taxi & Limousine Commission, as a principal product manager on Ad Hoc’s delivery and capture teams, and as COO and CPO at mobility startups.

Offers

How I work with firms

Solutioning support

2–6 weeks, during a bid.

Management consulting and subject matter expertise for your solution team: the implementation approach, the operating model, and an evaluator’s review of the draft.

Discovery

About 3 months.

Product discovery: user and stakeholder research, data analysis, and process mapping. Your firm delivers findings, a concept of operations, and a prioritized roadmap that says what to build, what not to build, and in what order.

Project startup (mobilization)

The first 60–90 days after award.

Operations setup: the operating model, transition plan, change management plan, and governance, handed to your delivery lead. Also available in the weeks around a major launch.

Recovery assessment

2–6 weeks.

An independent review of a struggling project, with a recovery plan your team carries out. You see the findings first and decide what the client sees.

Longer roles

I’ll consider a part-time product or operations lead role on a longer engagement, with a set term. I don’t take full-time roles.
