The work

What the work looks like

The bid with a builder behind it

The RFP is live and the solution keeps reading like everyone else’s. I join the war room with your solution team, and we shape an approach someone has actually built before. Then I read the draft the way the evaluation panel will. The orals get a product lead who can answer the technical question plainly.

The counterpart in the room

Your account director knows the client, the history, the politics. I take the other chair: requirements, feasibility, the straight answer when the client’s CTO pushes. The relationship stays with your lead.

The delivery that feels red

The status report says green, and your delivery lead isn’t sleeping. Point me at it for a read: two weeks inside the workstream, with the team and the artifacts. You get the findings first, and you decide what the client sees.

The first call

Two questions: which account is on your mind, and what seat needs filling. Thirty minutes, no pitch. If it’s not a fit, I’ll say so, and point you to someone who can help.
